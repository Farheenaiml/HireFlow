Interview notes - Backend Engineer panel, 45 minutes.

Python and REST APIs: Walked through the shipment tracking service. He built the REST APIs in Python with Flask, around 30 endpoints, and owns the request validation layer. Clear and specific here.

PostgreSQL: Described tuning slow queries on the shipments table. Added composite indexes and used EXPLAIN ANALYZE to cut a report query from 6 seconds to under one second. Has not done schema design from scratch.

Payments and ledgers: Asked about financial correctness. He said the billing module generated invoices and he has worked on some money related logic. Could not explain how rounding or idempotency was handled.

Production ownership: He is part of the on-call rotation. Mentioned handling incidents but did not describe a specific incident or what changed afterwards.

Event-driven systems: Introduced RabbitMQ for notifications. Answer was short, said it worked fine and there were no issues. Did not talk about retries, ordering or dead letter queues.

Mentoring: Reviews pull requests for two junior developers on the team.

Not discussed: written design documents, Kubernetes, compliance environments.
